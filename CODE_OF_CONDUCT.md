# Code of Conduct

PROOF is committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, constructive, and patient.
- Discuss ideas and evidence without attacking people.
- Respect differing backgrounds, identities, experiences, and viewpoints.
- Give and accept feedback in good faith.
- Protect private information and respect reasonable boundaries.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, deliberate intimidation, sustained disruption, and publication of another person's private information are not acceptable.

## Scope

This code applies in project repositories, reviews, issues, discussions, events, and other spaces where someone represents the project.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately using GitHub's private reporting features when available. If they are unavailable, contact a repository maintainer privately through GitHub. Do not include sensitive details in a public issue.

Maintainers will review reports as confidentially and promptly as practical. They may remove content, issue a warning, restrict participation, or ban a participant depending on the nature and pattern of the behavior. Retaliation against a reporter or participant in an investigation is prohibited.
